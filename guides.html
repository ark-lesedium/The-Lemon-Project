Kitchen Lemon Achaar (Pickle) — 5-Minute Method

+

You'll need

  • 15 lemons
  • 3 cups sugar
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tbsp cumin (jeera) seeds
  • 3 tbsp finely ground red chillies
  • 1½ tbsp salt
  • Optional: 2 tbsp methi masala or achaar masala

Steps

  1. Place the lemons in a dish, cover with hot water and leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Drain and dry the lemons thoroughly. Cut them into pieces and remove the seeds.
  3. In a separate pot, boil the sugar and water together for 15 minutes to make a syrup.
  4. Add the cumin seeds, red chillies, salt and optional masala to the cut lemons.
  5. Allow the syrup to cool slightly, then combine it with the seasoned lemons.
  6. Transfer into clean, sterilised containers. Store safely and refrigerate after opening.

Use clean utensils and food-safe containers. Label each batch with the preparation date. Recipe from "The Sour Lemon" skills training programme — Empowerment Projects, Miftahuddin Islamic Institute.

Cleaning All-Purpose Lemon Cleaning Spray

+

You'll need

  • Peels from 2–3 lemons
  • White vinegar, enough to cover peels
  • A clean glass jar with lid
  • Spray bottle
  • Water to dilute (1:1)

Steps

  1. Pack the lemon peels into the jar.
  2. Pour vinegar over the peels until fully submerged.
  3. Seal and store in a dark cupboard for 1–2 weeks, shaking every couple of days.
  4. Strain the liquid into your spray bottle, discarding the peels.
  5. Dilute 1:1 with water before use on counters, glass & sinks.
  6. Avoid use on natural stone (marble, granite) — the acid can etch the surface.

Preserving Salt-Preserved Lemons (Moroccan Style)

+

You'll need

  • 4–5 small lemons, washed
  • 4–5 tbsp coarse salt
  • A sterilised glass jar with tight lid
  • Extra fresh lemon juice to top up
  • Optional: bay leaf, cinnamon stick, peppercorns

Steps

  1. Cut each lemon into quarters, stopping just short of the base so it stays attached.
  2. Pack the inside of each lemon generously with salt.
  3. Press lemons firmly into the jar, layering with more salt as you go.
  4. Add optional spices, then top up with fresh lemon juice until lemons are fully covered.
  5. Seal and leave at room temperature for 3–4 weeks, shaking the jar daily for the first week.
  6. Once softened, refrigerate and use the rind in tagines, dressings & grain dishes for months.

Beauty Lemon Sugar Body Scrub

+

You'll need

  • 1 cup white or brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup olive or sunflower oil
  • Zest & juice of 1 lemon
  • A jar with a lid

Steps

  1. Mix sugar and oil together in a bowl until it forms a wet, sandy texture.
  2. Stir in lemon zest and a small squeeze of juice.
  3. Spoon into a jar and store at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
  4. Massage a small amount onto damp skin in circular motions, then rinse.
  5. Patch test first, and avoid direct sun exposure on treated skin for a few hours.

Cleaning Descale a Kettle With Lemon

+

You'll need

  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • Water
  • Your kettle

Steps

  1. Fill the kettle halfway with water and add the lemon slices.
  2. Bring to a boil, then switch off and let sit for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Pour out the water and wipe the interior with a soft cloth or brush to lift loosened scale.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water, then boil once more with plain water and discard before your next cup.

Kitchen Freeze Lemon Juice & Zest For Later

+

You'll need

  • As many lemons as you have on hand
  • Ice cube tray
  • Small freezer bags or containers
  • A fine grater for zest

Steps

  1. Zest each lemon before juicing and set the zest aside.
  2. Juice all the lemons, straining out pips.
  3. Pour juice into an ice cube tray and freeze until solid.
  4. Pop out the cubes into a labelled freezer bag — one cube ≈ one tablespoon.
  5. Freeze the zest flat in a small bag, then break off pieces as needed.
  6. Use frozen cubes straight in drinks, dressings & sauces — no need to thaw first.

Fragrance Stovetop Lemon Potpourri

+

You'll need

  • 1–2 lemons, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • A few whole cloves
  • Sprig of rosemary (optional)
  • Small pot of water

Steps

  1. Fill a small pot two-thirds with water.
  2. Add lemon slices, cinnamon stick, cloves and rosemary.
  3. Bring to a gentle simmer on the stovetop — never boil dry, top up water as needed.
  4. Let it simmer on low for a few hours to naturally scent your home.
  5. Turn off the stove when you leave the room or go to sleep.
